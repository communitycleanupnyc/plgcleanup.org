title: Guide
description: How to lead volunteers for a neighborhood cleanup.
---

You’re the Leader! You greet volunteers, run the intro, and answer questions during the clean. You're the face of the clean today. Be welcoming, keep it moving, have fun out there.

⚠️ EMERGENCY PROTOCOL: Message [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/H9her7r8N2) in case of injury or incident. Serious injury or emergency: call 911, then message [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/H9her7r8N2).

🌧️ WEATHER: Organizers will cancel via Discord for lightning or severe weather. Otherwise, the shift goes on.

## ⏰ Before the clean (morning of the cleanup → start)

- Message on Discord if you haven't received the key code to the combination lock at Maple St Community Garden. 
- Go to the garden, use the code you have been given to unlock the gate.
- Use the maps application on your phone to plan the route to the cleanup intersection start. Add 5 additional minutes to the walking estimate, given you will be moving at the pace of the cart. Aim to arrive 5 minutes early.
- If you are co-leading, find your co-lead and say hi!
- Start a 50-minute timer at the top of the hour, before intros. It will alert you with 10 minutes left.

## 🤝 First 10 minutes (intros)

Welcome everyone. Run intros: name, pronouns, ask an icebreaker (like, “what brought you out today?”).

Read the Community Agreements out loud to the group:

  - We clean from a place of mutual care and respect for the environment. We don’t police neighbors’ choices or trash habits.
  - We are open to all community members, and talk and share with anyone we meet.
  - We are an inclusive space: we share names and pronouns, and accommodate access needs.

State the ground rules: 
- Wear gloves.
- Skip dead animals, poop, needles, and broken glass (you as the Leader are the designated glass collector).
- Be aware of foot traffic around you.

Tell volunteers: _When your bag is full, bring it to me and I'll dump it into the big bag on the cart._

If you are working with a co-lead, they will hand out gloves, grabbers, and bags.

Extend our invite: Have fun! Say that if someone is intersted in becoming a clean lead, they should talk to you after. Invite everyone to join our Discord and follow us on Instagram, and say go to [plgcleanup.org](/) for the details. If there is a social event happening after, mention this now.

## 🚶 Middle ~40 minutes (the clean)

- Lead the route and move the cart along with the group toward the drop-off location. If a volunteer can't walk the full route, pair them with a buddy and assign a shorter stretch near the cart.
- You are the designated glass collector. Only you pick up broken glass using the broom and dustpan.
- As volunteers bring you full bags, thank them, dump the bag into the big bag on the cart, and fist-bump or high-five if that's your vibe.
- When a big bag on the cart is full, tie it off and set it at the end corner of the current block to weigh later. Ask a volunteer to help carry if it’s heavy. 
- If you finish your streets before the 50-minute timer, keep cleaning near the end corner until time's up.

## 🎁 Last 10 minutes (wrapping up)

- Bring the cart to the shift's end corner
- Call the group to the end corner. Have them dump their last bags into the big bags on the cart.

## 🧤 Handing out gloves

During nitros, open the storage container for the gloves. Remove the bags the volunteers will use from the cart. After introductions, hand out one pair of gloves, one picker, and one bag to each volunteer.

## ⏳ Latecomers

Latecomers: Give them gloves/picker/bag, point out the route, and give them the 30-second version of the ground rules (skip glass/needles/poop, bags to the Leader).

## 🏋️‍♀️ Weighing the trash (the weigh-in)

- Ask a volunteer: "Can you open the calculator on your phone and add up weights as I call them out?"
- Find the digital scale in the glove box in the cart.
- Weigh each big bag on the cart (plus any bags volunteers are still holding). Call out each weight; the volunteer adds them up. Weighing can run a few minutes past the hour—that's fine.
- Log the total weight in the Trash Tracker spreadsheet (you should have a link to this, message on Discord if not).
- **Yell out the total to your team and celebrate how much trash you collected!** Invite them to celebrate at a social event if one is happening post-cleanup, or invite them to grab a quick coffee at a nearby café if you feel inclined.
- Make sure all trash bags are placed at the drop-off corner at the end of your route. The corner can be found on the Trash Tracker spreadsheet.

## 📸 Group photo 

- After the trash has been weighed, invite everyone to be part of the group photo. Say it will be shared on social media, and that anyone can opt out. 
- Take the group photo.

## 🛒 Returning the cart 
- Return the cart to the Maple Street Community Garden.
- Put the tarp back on the cart to protect it from rain.
- Ensure the combination lock on the gate to the garden is locked as you exit.

## 💬 Post-cleanup communications

- Open Discord and post a summary message in the [#cleanup-photos-and-stories](https://discord.com/channels/1265073595496009869/1265081161487483042) channel, describing:
  - how many volunteers were on your team
  - how many pounds of trash you collected
  - any notable pieces of [lost treasure](/lost-treasure) your team found!
