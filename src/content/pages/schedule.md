# Upcoming cleanups

We clean every weekend in Prospect Lefferts Gardens, and we pick a different
route each time so the whole neighborhood gets a turn. A cleanup runs about an
hour. There's no registration and no sign-up sheet — you find the corner, you
show up, we hand you a grabber and a bag.

## Where to find the next one

The **[join page](/join)** always shows the next cleanup: the date, the time,
the street corner, and a map. It's the source of truth, and it updates as soon
as the next route is set.

For everything further out, pick whichever of these you'll actually read:

- [Our newsletter](https://communitycleanupplg.substack.com/subscribe) — the
  schedule by email, including any weather cancellations.
- [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/communitycleanupplg) — the route for the
  weekend, plus photos from the last one.
- [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/H9her7r8N2) — the #general channel, where
  the schedule gets posted and where you can ask if you're not sure you're in
  the right place.

## Trashy hours

Once in a while we skip the sidewalk and meet at a local business we
[partner](/partners) with instead. Those get announced in the newsletter and on
Instagram like any other cleanup.
