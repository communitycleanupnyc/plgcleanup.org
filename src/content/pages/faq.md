# FAQ

Have questions besides these? [Email us](mailto:communitycleanupplg@gmail.com)
or ask in the #general channel on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/H9her7r8N2).

## Do I need to register?

No. Just show up — all supplies are provided. Find the [next cleanup](/join).

## How can I nominate a cleanup route? 

If you know of a block that needs care and attention, [email us](mailto:communitycleanupplg@gmail.com), message us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/communitycleanupplg), or send a message on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/H9her7r8N2).

## Can I earn community service hours?

Yes. We sign school, DOE, and National Honor Society forms on the spot at every
cleanup. See [community service hours in Brooklyn](/community-service-hours) for
how it works.
