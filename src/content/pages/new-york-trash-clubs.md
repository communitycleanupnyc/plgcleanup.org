## Fort Tilden / Breezy Point Beach Cleanup
Times: Mornings, every Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.

Join the Signal Messenger group stay up to date by [clicking here](https://signal.group/#CjQKILnaeGPxS5YPp-9SMt-mHKY1FJEJg5j6KHPWzjdcpnbkEhB5ai-BIP05_tSlb1g_ajSr).

"Get outside, get some sun, help restore the habitat for the plovers, and meet new people offline! Leave feeling rejuvenated from soaking up nature with new friends!"

Bring sunscreen, water, snacks, any medicine you might need, and gear for cleaning, like gloves, trash bags, and hand sanitizer.

### Fort Tilden / Breezy Point Frequently Asked Questions
Q: "How is trash collected once we've finished cleaning up for the day?"

A: Trash is picked up regularly by NPS (National Park Service) workers from the bins on the main walking path.

Q: "What if I'm late?"

A: We all know trains & ferries can be hit or miss—particularly during the summer on those gorgeous days—so show up whenever you can and come look for us! If you can't find anyone, hey! make the most of it! You're at the beach, after all, right? :)

Q: "Are supplies for cleaning provided?"

A: "We're not a non-profit organization, so we can't supply the cleanup gear, unforch. We're just a bunch of people who have a love for the beach, who feel the urgency to preserve nature, and who are aiming to form new connection in their lives not mediated by social media! Don't hesitate to pester your local bar, restaurant, or grocery store for gloves & trash bags! Just tell them it's for a good cause! If you're taking the ferry, tell the ferry workers you're headed to a beach cleanup and ask if they can spare a set.
