Community Cleanup PLG is about more than picking up trash. It’s a chance to get outside, put the phone away for a while, and spend time with people who care about the place we call home. Community doesn’t just happen. You have to show up. In a time when it’s easy to feel disconnected, this is a simple way to be part of something that leaves our neighborhood a little better than we found it. We all leave with less trash and a little more hope.
