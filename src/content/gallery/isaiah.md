As someone who believes deeply in civic engagement and caring for your community, I think Community Cleanup PLG is a simple, easy, and meaningful way to get involved and make a difference without a major time commitment. Keeping our streets clean gives you the opportunity to meet your neighbors and build community in a way that's organic and unforced.

The best part about Cleanup is that it's for everyone. It's a great way for someone at the beginning of their civic engagement journey to get involved, for civic veterans looking for something new, or simply for those who want a more relaxed way to engage. At a time when there is a real hunger to participate civically while our society becomes increasingly isolated, Community Cleanup PLG creates an accessible and socially engaging entry point to civic life, while also supporting local businesses through partnerships like Trashy Hour and sponsored cleans.

Come explore parts of the community you haven't been to, try local business you haven't visited, and meet your neighbors!
