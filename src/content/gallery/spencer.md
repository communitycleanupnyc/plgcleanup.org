As a newcomer to Flatbush, it’s important to me that I participate as a neighbor. 

I think it’s easy to fall into the trap of believing that “making a difference” must be done at a macro level. But it can be as simple as picking up trash - it’s about respecting the neighborhood that I’m moving to, meeting neighbors and deepening connections. 

Keeping the sidewalks and streets clear of garbage helps prevent flooding due to blocked storm drains and helps people navigate the sidewalks more easily. 

Community Cleanup has been a great way to meet folks that live on my block and in the greater neighborhood, and it’s always uplifting to hear the sincere gratitude of neighbors.
