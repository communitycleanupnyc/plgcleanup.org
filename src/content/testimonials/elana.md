It was through Trash Club that I felt like I was able to feel a sense of real community here after having moved to the neighborhood. Picking up trash is the smallest part of it. I strongly believe in the necessity of built-in social routine; I think that is part of what defines a community—not one-off events here and there. The fact this club meets weekly if not two times per week is a true testament to this. And it’s easy in NYC to fall into an exhausting pattern of every social plan requiring coordination. Routines are really important: I want to be able to regularly be with people I like spending time with, without so much investment in coordination or travel/food/etc. expenses. So yeah… come fiddle around with those trash grabby claws with us!
